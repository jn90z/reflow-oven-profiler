# Mechanical and thermal requirements

Oven: **Heller 1809 MK7**, with both **edge-rail/chain** and **mesh-belt** conveyor configurations.

- Reusable sealed electronics logger in an insulated, low-profile stainless-steel barrier.
- Interchangeable mesh-belt carrier and edge-rail carrier; no loose hardware, protrusions, snagging edges or unsupported overhang.
- Preliminary envelope goal 120 x 80 x 25 mm **not validated** and may be incompatible with thermal protection. Obtain physical machine clearances and rail geometry before CAD.
- Type K thermocouple harness travels with an instrumented PCB, with positive strain relief and protected pass-through.
- Avoid metal shields immediately around antenna; no assumed Wi-Fi connectivity inside enclosed tunnel. RF-window or antenna schemes require heat and RF qualification.
- First test thermal barrier with a passive dummy load and independent internal temperature measurement, including post-exit heat soak and stopped-conveyor risk.
- No battery, microcontroller or ordinary 3D-printed material inside the oven during preliminary enclosure testing.
- Select insulation with suitable thermal properties and safe handling / containment documentation.
- Verify conveyor carrier mass, center of gravity, edge-rail engagement and cool-down handling before use.

### Open measurements
Actual conveyor entry clearance, minimum rail pitch, typical profiler PCB width, tunnel profile/exposure duration, carrier height and permitted loading.
